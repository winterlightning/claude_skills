# Mother-and-daughter alternatives

The original SVG is retained in original.svg and is verified unchanged. These are independent registered variants v2–v4, shown as A–C in the comparison page.

- A: rounded overlapping hoods and a taller adult behind the child; separate circular face openings replace the previous child dot.
- B: larger circular faces and triangular draped scarves; a staggered silhouette emphasizes the difference in height.
- C: larger circular faces brought closer together, shorter shoulder drapes and open bust outlines.

The natural pair is deliberately asymmetric. Face radii are 5/4 for A and 8/6 for B/C; the paired scarf endpoints and their occlusions are shared explicitly. All face outlines use circular arcs. Face openings in continuous headscarves are not detached stick-figure heads. Contacts are actual temple/scarf joins or garment occlusion boundaries.

References inspected: original supplied SVG; icon_set/references/human_ref/user.svg (circular heads, broad shoulders and open bust); local Lucide original and atomic-debug users-round.svg (overlapping figures and reduced contours).

The user’s existing permission to resize beyond standard keyshapes is retained in per-variant FREE records. Stroke, MIC, hole and canvas rules remain unchanged. All three pass full per-icon QA with zero errors and warnings and are visually inspected enlarged/native in both themes. B and C communicate faces more strongly than A; C is the suggested direction for further review. No original replacement is selected automatically.
