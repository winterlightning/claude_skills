# Container content generation

The user explicitly authorized generating complete combined subjects and using current system artwork as reference. The original 51 source IDs are retained in `generation-status.json`, in model `SOURCE_ICON_ID` / `SOURCE_REFERENCES`, and in text manifest source links.

## Artwork decisions

- Reused visually checked SOLO48 artwork: heart pulse; tombstone with cross; wrench with lightning; zoom in; zoom out. Reuse adds source links without changing their geometry.
- Other subjects are authored as complete SOLO48 models. No family profile, keyshape, clearance threshold, or validation waiver was changed.
- Construction references were the local Lucide original and atomic-debug examples for triangle-alert, user-round-plus, file-up, battery-charging, plug-zap, scan-face, clapperboard, badge-check, paw-print, car, wrench, shirt, chart-pie, and delete.
- Human busts use a circular head of radius 6, centered at y=14, and shoulders whose top is y=28: 8 units between centerlines, or 4 units between ink edges. The shared human reference informed these proportions. Female hair extends from the head sides rather than replacing the circular jaw. The height-measurement figure retains its earlier exact 4-unit detached head/body gap.
- The two car/wrench references retain opposite arrangements. The source labelled “Bed with Key” visibly depicts a front-view car with a key, so its artwork preserves that visible subject.
- The plug's bolt was moved beside the plug, and the clapperboard was made taller, to preserve readable details at stroke 4. The shirt neckline was narrowed to leave clear sleeves. Nonessential headlights were omitted from the compact car/wrench subjects.
- Arrow and tool directions remain deliberately asymmetric. Portraits, paired wheel stems, and paired paw toes use shared dimensions.

## Text and symbols

Question mark, percentage, PM / 2.5, paragraph T, plus/minus layout, and 16:9 reuse the existing typeface. PM / 2.5 and plus/minus have two lines with smaller glyphs; the entire layout is at most 32 units of visible ink high. Natural glyph proportions and effective stroke width 4 are preserved. Width is not forced into a SOLO48 keyshape.

The complete-subject instruction also covered the previously missing symbols. Membership, yen, and Om were authored as symbol artwork. Fahrenheit reuses the existing F glyph with a new degree circle. The car-dollar layout reuses the existing dollar glyph and the existing plain-car-front artwork, arranged side by side so the dollar stays readable at stroke 4. The text compositions are exported under `dist/text44`, with normal gallery source links.

## Horizontal dots

The unframed horizontal ellipsis is now generated from three existing `symbol-period` glyphs, with 20-unit center spacing, 4-unit stroke, and natural 4-unit ink height centered in a 44-unit canvas. This follows the existing typeface policy and the user's maximum-height request. Its older SOLO48 model and invalid keyshape findings are retained for history; that model is not misrepresented as passing. The source now links to the valid text layout.

## Checks

Each authored/reused model is checked with the unmodified validator. Targeted builds also check internal spacing, holes and pinches. Light and dark contact sheets show all 51 results at native and enlarged sizes. `generation-status.json` records each final output and release/review state.

The text-family tests pass. Broader schema checks reported failures in unrelated letter-C/number-5 and zero-percent models. The older renderer test module could not import the removed `icon_set.model.icons.sub.shapes` module. These failures were not bypassed or repaired as part of this batch.

## Composition dimension review

Measured all 51 original and generated SVGs: canvas size, visible ink width/height, and aspect ratio. See dimension-audit.html and dimension-audit.json. Ink measurements are rendered bounds, with 1/16-unit sampling for 48-unit assets and up to 2048 pixels for large originals. Aspect differences over 20% are review flags, not automatic validation failures. Different source canvas sizes make raw pixel sizes unsuitable for direct fitting; compare ink aspect ratios and preserve uniform scaling.

Four independently saved revisions replace the earlier drawings in this experiment:
- Warning triangle: SQUARE, 40 × 40 ink. Wider triangle with centered exclamation.
- Plug with lightning: VRECT_L, 36 × 44 ink. Lightning restored inside a full-size plug body.
- Charging battery: HRECT_M, 44 × 32 ink. Closed horizontal battery with interior lightning.
- Disabled battery: HRECT_M, 44 × 32 ink. Complete battery body with corner-to-corner slash; disconnected wall fragments removed.

These retain SOLO48 keyshape and 4-unit stroke constraints; they are not exact copies of the original aspect ratios. Parent drawings are preserved. The original supplied drawings determined symbol placement; local Lucide battery-charging and plug-zap informed contour simplicity, but their different layouts were not used as replacements for the supplied subjects.

Full export review: triangle and disabled-battery revisions pass. The enclosed plug and charging-battery revisions remain REVIEW drafts: the lightning strokes leave 1.6562 and 1.7719 units of visible clearance respectively, below the required 4. Initial model validation alone did not catch the export's internal-spacing check. These drafts must not be treated as combination-ready. No validators or spacing thresholds were changed.
