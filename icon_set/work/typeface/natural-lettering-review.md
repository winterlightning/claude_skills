# Natural-proportion lettering revision

The active Text combine and Experiment → Typeface collections now use `icon_set/typeface/glyphs.json`, regenerated by `icon_set/scripts/build_typeface.py`. They no longer use SOLO48 geometry or keyshape fitting. The older icon models remain historical records; this revision does not alter profile rules.

## What changed

- All 37 references retain their natural width/height relationships through a uniform transform, with source cubic curve structure preserved wherever sound.
- Restored compact r shoulders and j hooks, round c/e bowls, natural s waist, broad low m arches, and rounded lower turns on 2.
- Removed subpixel exporter fragments, duplicate h/m stem runs, and stray dot offsets. Rebuilt 0 as a coherent elliptical capsule and v with smooth joined curves. Snapped k/y branch endpoints to their receiving strokes.
- Reconstructed the filled-outline e as adjustable centerline strokes, preserving its circular bowl and open terminal.
- Re-authored A–Z with independent natural widths, smooth bowls, compact I/J, and matching rounded styling. No original uppercase source was supplied.
- Body heights, cap heights and baselines still control text alignment; letter and line spacing, stroke width, and clean SVG download remain available.

## Evidence and checks

Inspected every source pair in `natural-comparison-1.png` through `natural-comparison-3.png`, and all capitals in `natural-alphabet.png`. Mixed-case text was inspected in both light and dark themes (`natural-final.png`, `natural-final-dark.png`).

Fourteen focused Python tests and the JavaScript layout/export suite pass. Checks cover source provenance, preservation of natural aspect ratios, all 63 glyph bounds and body metrics, complete character coverage, unclipped previews, curved r/j/s/e/2 shapes, original comparisons, multiline baseline alignment, and uniform exported stroke weights from 0.5 to 16. These are lettering checks, not SOLO48 validation claims.

Individual editable SVGs and their manifest are exported to `icon_set/dist/gallery/typeface/`. The comparison gallery embeds its reference previews and displays Original / Iconized / Centerline. The j body baseline remains a documented optical estimate; its source curve and compact hook are retained. Lettering quality remains subject to visual judgment, rather than a claim of universal perfection.
