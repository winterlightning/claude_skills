# Fifty sub-icon repairs

All 50 selected models failed only their declared envelope. All 50 corrected variants pass full artwork QA and composition checks. Originals remain unchanged. Before/after sheets were inspected at native and enlarged sizes in both themes. No profile constants, thresholds, strokes or human approvals changed.

References: local Lucide arrow-left, bell, book-open and shopping-cart originals and atomic geometry; principles used were coherent joined shafts, mirrored silhouettes, shared attachment points and repeated spacing. No useful exact match was needed for the remaining small envelope repairs.

Four inherited concept labels are more specific than their visible forms (Bitcoin graph, camera-video, Cao Dai, chart); their source depictions were preserved and the semantic uncertainty is recorded rather than claimed resolved by numeric QA.
