# monitor graduation hat

Kept the monitor, stand and mortarboard silhouette. Simplified the shallow crown into a mortarboard with a short tassel.

Construction reference: local Lucide `graduation-cap` original and atomic-debug; used its basic silhouette and attachment structure, re-authored at SOLO48.

Keyshape: SQUARE, ink (4,4)-(44,44), fits the screen and centered stand.

Visual review: checked at 48 and 240 pixels in light and dark themes. Symmetric enclosures stay centered; directional symbols retain deliberate asymmetry.
