# Mobile Phone Cube

Enlarged all three cube faces and retained the perspective seams. Removed the lower phone divider and used a square-cornered phone outline to preserve eight-unit clearance.

Construction reference: local Lucide `box` original and atomic-debug; used its basic silhouette and attachment structure, re-authored at SOLO48.

Keyshape: VRECT_L, ink (6,2)-(42,46), fits the upright phone or document.

Visual review: checked at 48 and 240 pixels in light and dark themes. Symmetric enclosures stay centered; directional symbols retain deliberate asymmetry.
