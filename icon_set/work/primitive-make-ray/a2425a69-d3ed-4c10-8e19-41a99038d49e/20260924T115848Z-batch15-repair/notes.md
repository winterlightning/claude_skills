# money bill pound

Kept the banknote enclosure and sterling denomination; removed four decorative corner loops to reserve room for the currency sign.

Construction reference: local Lucide `pound-sterling` original and atomic-debug; used its basic silhouette and attachment structure, re-authored at SOLO48.

Keyshape: HRECT_L, ink (2,6)-(46,42), fits the horizontal banknote.

Visual review: checked at 48 and 240 pixels in light and dark themes. Symmetric enclosures stay centered; directional symbols retain deliberate asymmetry.
