# mobile phone music note

Retained the round note head, upright stem and single flag; shortened the flag to clear the head.

Construction reference: local Lucide `music` original and atomic-debug; used its basic silhouette and attachment structure, re-authored at SOLO48.

Keyshape: VRECT_L, ink (6,2)-(42,46), fits the upright phone or document.

Visual review: checked at 48 and 240 pixels in light and dark themes. Symmetric enclosures stay centered; directional symbols retain deliberate asymmetry.
