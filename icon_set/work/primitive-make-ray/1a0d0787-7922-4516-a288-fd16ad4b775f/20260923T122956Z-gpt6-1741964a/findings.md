# monitor with F

A monitor displaying the uppercase letter F.

Keyshape: VRECT_L — Portrait screen gives the nested symbol enough vertical space. Exact centerline extremes are recorded in the Python module.

Construction: Shared monitor and a hand-authored F made from a split upright and two horizontal bars.

Visual review: Light/dark previews inspected at native and enlarged sizes. F has distinct bars with the required separation. A taller monitor provides room for the lower stem; letter and stand share a balanced visual centre.

Omissions and reductions: Monitor made taller to give its content more room..
