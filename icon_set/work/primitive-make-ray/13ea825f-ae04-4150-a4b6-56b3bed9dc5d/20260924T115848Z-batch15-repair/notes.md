# mobile phone phone

Retained the curved telephone receiver with two turned terminals; reduced its crowded double outline to an open receiver stroke.

Construction reference: local Lucide `phone` original and atomic-debug; used its basic silhouette and attachment structure, re-authored at SOLO48.

Keyshape: VRECT_L, ink (6,2)-(42,46), fits the upright phone or document.

Visual review: checked at 48 and 240 pixels in light and dark themes. Symmetric enclosures stay centered; directional symbols retain deliberate asymmetry.
