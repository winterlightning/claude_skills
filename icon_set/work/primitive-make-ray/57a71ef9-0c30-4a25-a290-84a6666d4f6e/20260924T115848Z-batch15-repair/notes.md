# money bill

Kept all four banknote corner ornaments and the central medallion; enlarged corner radii from eight to ten.

Construction reference: local Lucide `banknote` original and atomic-debug; used its basic silhouette and attachment structure, re-authored at SOLO48.

Keyshape: HRECT_L, ink (2,6)-(46,42), fits the horizontal banknote.

Visual review: checked at 48 and 240 pixels in light and dark themes. Symmetric enclosures stay centered; directional symbols retain deliberate asymmetry.
