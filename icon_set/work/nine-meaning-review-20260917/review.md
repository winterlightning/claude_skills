# Review of nine revised icons

Reviewed fresh Python-model renders at native 48 px and enlarged scale in light/dark themes, plus all nine source references. The misspelled `erson-receiving-cheek-massage` is interpreted as the registered `person-receiving-cheek-massage`.

**Verdict: all nine need another design pass.** Six have weak/mismatched meaning; the two paw prints and perching bird communicate their broad concept but still need drawing repairs. All nine currently pass numeric QA. That pass does not establish visual readiness; the earlier batch review was too permissive.

This is a review only. No icon source, export or production status was changed. Proposed constructions below are not yet implemented or validated. Existing user authorization for custom keyshape proportions and in-place originals remains part of the design brief.

## Findings

| Icon | Meaning | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| [cough](cough/review.md) | weak | The head has no identifiable open mouth. cough-upper and cough-lower do not visibly originate from a mouth; the face/nose/jaw are an angular staircase. |
| [paw-print](paw-print/review.md) | convincing | pad changes tangent at its two lower outer shoulders, producing matching visible bumps. Circular upper/outer toes read as beads instead of soft pads, and the widely splayed outer toes weaken the compact footprint silhouette. |
| [paw-print-small-outer-toes](paw-print-small-outer-toes/review.md) | convincing | The two radius-2 outer circles have zero nominal hole diameter under the 4-unit stroke and therefore read solid. The radius-3 inner circles retain tiny holes. pad has the same mirrored tangent kinks as paw-print. |
| [peacock-with-spread-tail](peacock-with-spread-tail/review.md) | weak | fan has a flat upper stretch and stepped shoulders instead of a radiating feather fan. body is a perfect ring, neck a straight stalk, feet an X-like fork. eye-l=(11,22) and eye-r=(37,24) are two units out of level despite symmetric fan geometry. |
| [pelican-on-water](pelican-on-water/review.md) | weak | bird has no eye and an open/incomplete belly. The neck turns directly into a narrow upward-pointing crescent, leaving little distinct torso. bill has a nearly vertical rear edge and oversized bowl-shaped pouch. water is exactly straight, not a ripple. |
| [perching-bird](perching-bird/review.md) | convincing | bird uses a long straight diagonal from the crown area to the tail tip. wing is only a tiny bent dab. leg meets the breast at a visible bump and continues as one thick support into a long branch. |
| [person-receiving-cheek-massage](person-receiving-cheek-massage/review.md) | mismatch | hand has a horizontal top and two vertical sides forming a rectangular block, with a small inward hook. It joins face at the outer side but does not create an identifiable palm/thumb contact on the cheek. Straight eye dashes provide little relaxed-expression cue. |
| [oval-stadium-with-two-flags](oval-stadium-with-two-flags/review.md) | weak | flag-* consists only of a pole and one sloping stroke, so no pennant area exists. The tall poles dominate the shallow rim. rim and wall read like household bowl edges; poles are related to rim without shared attachment endpoints. |
| [oval-stadium-with-three-flags](oval-stadium-with-three-flags/review.md) | weak | flag-* consists only of a pole and one sloping stroke, so no pennant area exists. The tall poles dominate the shallow rim. rim and wall read like household bowl edges; poles are related to rim without shared attachment endpoints. |

## Priority

Redraw the massage gesture and peacock first, then pelican and both stadiums; clarify cough; polish the paw pair and perching bird.
