## Measurement rules

- Dimensions below are width × height in SVG grid units, rounded to three decimals for reporting only.
- Path bounds measure the centerlines; they exclude stroke thickness.
- The stroke envelope adds the default 4-unit round stroke to both dimensions. This is the bounding-envelope convention used by the text layout.
- Body height is the typographic body band, so dots, ascenders, descenders, and punctuation may extend outside it.
- Preview exports are displayed at 48 × 48, but their viewBox may expand. This does not make the letter itself 48 units high.
- The current geometry policy preserves proportions without whole-unit grid fitting.
- The text composer defaults to a 36-unit lowercase body and 52-unit capital/digit body, with 4-unit stroke. These differ from stored base coordinates.
