## Visual draft

A large crescent moon sits above a thin horizontal horizon line. A separate upward arrow rises through the open lower crescent, with its shaft extending down toward the horizon beneath it.

## 1. Crescent Moon above Horizon (solo)

A large crescent opening toward the upper right above a separate horizontal horizon line. Exclude the upward arrow.

## 2. Moonrise Up Arrow (sub)

An upward arrow with a straight vertical shaft and two open diagonal head branches. Exclude the moon and horizon.
