Container center audit — 210 containers

Blue cross: chosen center. Red ring: previous point. Purple ring: center of a largest fitting circle. Coincident markers may overlap. Every card shows enlarged and native 64-unit previews.

Definition: center means the balance point (area centroid) of the selected content zone, rounded to a half unit. If the centroid is outside the zone, use a largest-circle center. The purple point answers a different question: where a round sub icon could be largest. Equal-radius circle candidates are resolved toward the centroid. A large disagreement lowers confidence and calls for checking the actual paired icon.

Evidence and confidence rubric

The /100 score is a transparent heuristic rating, not a measured probability or a user-study result. Source identity: 20; conservative raster zone clearance: 15; semantic boundary support: 20 for closed faces, 15 for manual open boundaries, capped at 12 for context-sensitive faces; agreement between centroid and largest-circle center: up to 20, minus 2 per unit of disagreement; symmetry support: 15 for two mirror axes, 10 for one, 5 otherwise; contained centroid: 10 (fallback: 4). High ≥90, Medium 75–89, Low <75. Low symmetry does not itself mean bad design; it means less geometric evidence for an unambiguous center.

Center-only overlays use a separate 85/100 rationale score and claim no safe area. Zone-boundary distances are measured on saved polygons. Mirror overlap compares the zone against its reflection about its bounds midpoint; ≥98% supports an axis. Placement review is distinct from full icon-model/build QA. Source SVGs are preserved.

All polygons still require actual sub-icon vector clearance checks. Green boundaries are the previously reviewed conservative zones; this audit corrects centers, not primitive shapes.
