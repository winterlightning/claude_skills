The preceding geometry-pass claim did not establish drawing quality. This review supersedes it. All 100 source/current comparisons were opened, along with native light/dark candidates and centerline sheets. 40 candidates were constructed; 38 selected after visual review and full QA. The glider candidate was rejected; its earlier drawing is retained. The five-bar waveform candidate remains unpublished pending interpretation/simplification. Ultrasound specificity and QR/barcode naming remain explicitly uncertain.

Lucide references inspected: eye-off, ice-cream-cone, cloud, feather, scissors, bug, dna and spline, including atomic-debug construction. Principles used: fewer coherent curves, balanced local geometry, natural aspect ratios and clear silhouettes. The source references remain available on every gallery card.
