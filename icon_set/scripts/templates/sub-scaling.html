Solo → Sub scaling

See which 48px solo icons are used as small components, and what happens when they fit inside a 32px ink box.

Proportions first, whole-number sizes. Automatic scaling chooses a whole-number ink box within 32×32, including the 4px stroke. It uses the largest exact-proportion size where possible. If no useful exact size exists, it rounds the shorter side and reports the small proportion change below. No SUB32 keyshape is imposed. Rounded dimensions do not mean every internal stroke coordinate is on the grid.

32px keyshapes

The same six shape choices as SOLO48, using the existing SUB32 dimensions. Circle and square share a 32×32 outer box, but the circle has a radial boundary. Blue is the ink boundary; dashed blue is the stroke centerline guide, inset by 2px.
