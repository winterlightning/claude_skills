Existing container and solo icons combined on a 64 × 64 canvas. These saved layouts are trial previews; the placement rules below guide new generations.

Placement rules and generation guide
  • Keep both strokes at 4 units, with at least 2 units between visible stroke edges.
  • Use the container’s reviewed content area and preferred center. Automatically detected areas need visual review.
  • Fit content proportionally, trying visible sizes of 32, 28, then 24 units. Search within 10 units of the preferred center.
  • Check clearance, canvas bounds, and fit. If nothing fits, flag the preview for review. Intentional overlays always need review.
  • Review the meaning and legibility of each combination. Existing previews retain their earlier placements until regenerated.
