Typeface / Text combine

Letters that belong together.

Combine letters by their body height and baseline. The bowl of “b” matches “o,” while its stem rises naturally above the line.

Uppercase A–Z, lowercase a–z, digits 0–9, spaces, and all standard keyboard symbols (such as ! @ # $ % & *). Press Enter for a new line. Version v2 renders every letter in uppercase from the UPPER drawings and borrows digits, symbols and any missing letter from v1.

See the inner body

The shaded band is the main body. The lower guide is the baseline. Dots and extending stems don’t determine the body size.
